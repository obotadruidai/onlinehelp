## Open a new ticket

If you want to open a new ticket, just type “I want to add a ticket”

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/See Ticket Management in action.png>)

The AI Agent will guide you through the ticket opening process:

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/See Ticket Management in action_1.png>)

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/See Ticket Management in action_2.png>)

After collecting all the information, an unattended UiPath process is triggered and the ticket will be registered in ServiceNow App.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/See Ticket Management in action_3.png>)

Please see below the ticket registered in ServiceNow:

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/See Ticket Management in action_4.png>)

## Add comment/photo to the ticket

Once you have opened a ticket, you can just type “my tickets” and you will have access to:

* see the status of all your tickets.
* add photos/comments on any opened ticket.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/See Ticket Management in action_5.png>)
