For medical providers

The **Lab Results Management** workspace allows staff to manage the Complete Lab Order lifecycle. This ensures that every lab order is tracked from generation through to the final check-up.

## Generate a Lab Order

Medical staff and doctors should perform the following steps only when a patient has honored their appointment:

1. Select the **Lab Results Management** workspace.
2. Click **Appointments** on the workspace menu.
3. Select the appointment which the patient honored. The appointment form opens.
4. Click the **Order Lab Tests** button. The Chat window opens on the right and goes straight into the guided flow.

![](<../../../../../../.gitbook/assets/How to Use the Solution (1).png>)

Once in the guided flow (from either path), the AI Agent gathers each test, its priority, and the lab order’s clinical reason.
