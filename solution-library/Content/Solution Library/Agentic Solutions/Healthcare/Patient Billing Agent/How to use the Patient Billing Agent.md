For medical providers

The Billing Management workspace allows staff to manage the Complete Billing lifecycle. This ensures that every payment order is tracked from generation through to the final payment.

## Generating a payment order

Medical staff and doctors should perform the following steps only when a patient has honored their appointment:

1. Select the **Billing Management** workspace.
2. Click **Appointments** on the workspace menu.
3. Select the appointment which the patient honored. The appointment form opens.
4. Click the **Complete Appointment** button at the top right corner of the form.

![](<../../../../../.gitbook/assets/complete appointment.png>)

{% hint style="info" %}
**Info:** If the button is inactive, reach out to your admin as they need to enable the Billing module.
{% endhint %}

5. The Chat window opens on the right to inform that the appointment was successfully completed and the payment order has been generated.

This action signifies that the medical staff / doctor has successfully provided the service and the visit is officially honored.

Close the chat window, then close the form.
