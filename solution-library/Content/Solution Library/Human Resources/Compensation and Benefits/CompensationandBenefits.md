This solution template provides a wide range of skills for HR Admins/Managers and employees:

### Health / Insurance Provider

Register in the HR system the employee's choice regarding the insurance or health subscription provider. Offer information regarding the actual insurance/ health policies.

### Retirement Benefits

Register in the HR system the employee's choice regarding the retirement benefits. Offer information regarding available plans for retirement.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/retirement benefits.png>)

### Non-financial benefits

Save in the HR system the employee's choice for non-financial benefits like gym, movie or escape rooms subscriptions. Offer detailed information about each non-financial benefit and activate it.

![](../../../../.gitbook/assets/nonfinancial.png)

### Rewards and Recognition

Using this functionality, as a Manager you will be able to propose new salary increases, bonuses or job promotion for each employee. Also, you can evaluate your employee’s basis on the list of questions defined in the evaluation tool.

You will be able to register the request in the HR business system and the HR Admin and Employee will receive a notification when a new evaluation was performed or when a new employee received a bonus.

![](../../../../.gitbook/assets/rewards.png)
