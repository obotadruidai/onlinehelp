<a id="QnA"></a>


The QnA is a special type of conversational flow designed to simplify the business user experience when creating simple conversations. They are comprised of training phrases (primary and alternate questions) and a single step of type Message that delivers the response to the user (answer).

Business users do not need any AI Agent technology understanding to create a collection of question-answer (QnA) pairs.

Business users do not need any AI Agent technology understanding.

<a id="Usecases"></a>

## Use cases

- Employee facing AI Agents maintained by HR (QnA about best practices, internal regulations, etc.)
- Customer facing AI Agents maintained by Public Relations, Support Center, Marketing

{% hint style="info" %}
**NOTE:**  To use the QnA, you need a Knowledgebase Author (KB Author) security role. If you don’t have one, get in touch with your Druid Portal administrator.
{% endhint %}

<a id="AccessingtheQnA"></a>

## Accessing the QnA

To access the QnA, select the desired AI Agent and solution and on the main menu, click **QnA**. The list of Questions and Answers defined in the current solution appears. The question-answer pairs are organized by categories.

![](../Resources/Images/QnA/ACCESS%20QNA.png)

The Categories expands by default. If you want to hide it, click the ![](../Resources/Images/QnA/hide%20categories.png) icon.

![](../Resources/Images/QnA/hidden%20categories.png)

To show the Categories, click the icon ![](../Resources/Images/QnA/show%20categories_23x20.png) .

Clicking on a category, you get the list of articles in that category. This is particularly useful when you have many articles in different categories and you’d like to revise the articles in a specific category.

![](../Resources/Images/QnA/qnas%20in%20a%20category.png)

Clicking on an article in a category shows the details of the selected knowledgebase article on the right giving allowing you the possibility to edit it while viewing the related articles in the same category. This is particularly useful when you need to update an article that requires updates of related articles.

![](../Resources/Images/QnA/selected%20qna.png)
