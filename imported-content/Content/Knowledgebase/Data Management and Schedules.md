# Data Management and Schedules

Connecting your data sources is only the first step; maintaining their freshness and monitoring their health ensures your AI Agent never serves stale or outdated information.

The Data Management & Schedules section provides the guides needed to keep your Knowledge Base synchronized and optimized. Here, you will find instructions on:

- Scheduling automatic syncs to fetch the latest updates from your connected data sources without manual intervention.
- Monitoring extraction logs to troubleshoot sync errors, track document processing status, and verify file parsing.
- Performing bulk actions to pause, resume, retrain, or delete multiple data feeds simultaneously.

Review these topics to establish a robust maintenance workflow for your AI Agent.
