<a id="ConversationHistory"></a>


Druid Conductor's Conversation History lets you trace the full journey of any conversation — including which orchestrated AI Agent responded to each message. Messages handled by an orchestrated AI Agent display its name.

![](../Resources/Images/Megabots/conv%20history.png)

To filter conversation history by a specific orchestrated AI Agent, click **More filters** and select the desired AI Agent from the **Orchestrated** AI Agents dropdown.

![](../Resources/Images/Megabots/more%20filters.png)

> **Info:** All Druid Conductor conversations involving an orchestrated AI Agent are also visible in that AI Agent's own Conversation History. For more information, see [Conversation History](../Data%20Analytics%20%26%20Insights/History%20%26%20Logs/Conversation%20History%20V2/Conversation%20History.md).
