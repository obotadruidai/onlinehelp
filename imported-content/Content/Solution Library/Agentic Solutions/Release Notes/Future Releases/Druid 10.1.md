## Healthcare

<a id="InsuranceManagementAgent"></a>

### Insurance Management Agent

The Insurance Agent expands the platform's insurance capabilities with two new skills focused on cost transparency and operational visibility — helping both patients and staff navigate insurance processes with greater confidence and efficiency.

- **Cost Estimates for Services Based on Insurance Verification Skill** - This skill enables the Insurance Agent to generate accurate, real-time cost estimates for upcoming services by cross-referencing the patient's verified insurance coverage against the provider's fee schedule. Delivering transparent cost information upfront reduces billing disputes and measurably improves patient trust and satisfaction.

## Insurance

<a id="OutboundCollectionAgent"></a>

### Outbound Collection Agent

- **Outbound campaigns for soft Collection Skill**. Introduces automated outbound collection campaigns tailored specifically for payment reminders and early-stage outstanding balances. This feature enables insurance carriers to maintain high policy retention rates through proactive, customer-friendly communication before coverage lapses occur.

## Operational Tools

<a id="AITestingAgent"></a>

### AI Testing Agent

Building an AI Agent is easy; proving it is accurate is where most teams struggle. Until now, validation meant manual spot-checks, user complaints, or slow, one-off reviews that couldn't scale. AI Testing Agent changes that by turning knowledge validation into a continuous, automated process.

Powered by AI, it automatically generates test cases directly from your knowledge sources and executes them at scale. This allows you to compare performance across different LLMs and prompts — identifying exactly where your AI Agent excels or fails before it ever touches a live user.

Purpose-built for every stage of the AI Agent lifecycle — from proving accuracy to clients during acceptance, to experimenting with model and prompt combinations, to enforcing governance in production. No manual reviews. No disruptive testing cycles. No blind spots. Just continuous knowledge testing helps you ship with confidence, refine without risk, and deliver an AI Agent that truly knows what it's talking about.
