<a id="CaseReportingDashboard"></a>


For helpdesk managers

The **Case Reporting v2** dashboard available in the **Helpdesk Dashboard** workspace, provides helpdesk managers with real-time operational insights and analytics regarding case volume, resolution status, classification, queue allocation, and SLA compliance.

Managers can filter data using the date range selector, apply custom filters, or export reporting data via the action bar at the top right of the page.

The dashboard features three main analytical views accessible via tabs:

- [Cases By Status](#Cases)
- [Cases By Category](#Cases2)
- [Cases By Queue and SLA](#Cases3)

<a id="CasesByStatus"></a>

## <a id="Cases"></a>Cases By Status

The **By Status** tab gives managers visibility into current case states and volume trends over time:

- **Open/Closed**. A pie chart displaying the overall proportion of open versus closed cases.
- **By Date And Status**. A stacked bar chart illustrating daily case volume broken down by case status (e.g., New, Closed By Agent).
- **Open By Date**. Highlights the number of new cases opened on specific dates.
- **Closed By Date**. Displays the count of cases closed on specific dates by support agents.

## <a id="Cases2"></a>Cases By Category

The By Category tab helps managers analyze ticket distribution across categories, subcategories, and priority levels to identify recurring issues or systemic problems:

- **By Date And Category**. A stacked bar chart showing ticket volume over time categorized by issue type (e.g., Network Issues, Software Issues).
- **By Category And Priority**. Breaks down ticket volume across categories by priority level (e.g., Critical, High).
- **By Category And Subcategory**. Maps ticket distribution across top-level categories and specific subcategories (e.g., Operating System, Peripheral Issue).

![](../../../../Resources/Images/Ticketron%20Final/by%20category_1656x801.png)

## <a id="Cases3"></a>Cases By Queue and SLA

The **By Queue and SLA** tab assists managers in evaluating queue workloads and tracking Service Level Agreement (SLA) performance:

- **By Date and Queue**. Displays daily ticket incoming volume assigned to specific support queues (e.g., Generic Support Queue).
- **By SLA**. Evaluates ticket volume against SLA compliance thresholds (e.g., SLA met vs. breached across service categories).
