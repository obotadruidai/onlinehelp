# Business Apps

Agentic Solutions combine Druid conversational AI and automation capabilities into ready-to-use business applications. They help you create intelligent, goal-oriented agents faster and with less manual setup.

Use a business app when you want to:

- **Accelerate implementation**. Start from a working business app instead of building an AI Agent from scratch.
- **Reduce development time**. Use predefined flows and AI configurations based on best practices.
- **Ensure consistency**. Apply the same conversational patterns across departments or products.
- **Simplify customization**. Adapt existing business apps to your company’s processes and branding.

Druid offers business apps across 4 major business domains: BFSI, Heathcare, Retail and Higher Education. It also offers operational business apps that streamline the authoring experience. Each business app is documented in a separate topic that explains how to configure and use it.
