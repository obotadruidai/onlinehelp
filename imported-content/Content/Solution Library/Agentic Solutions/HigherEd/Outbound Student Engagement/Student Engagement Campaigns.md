# Student Engagement Campaigns

for campaign managers

The Campaigns section in the **Campaign & Contacts** workspace contains all outbound campaigns. The solution includes four campaigns that are configured and ready to run out of the box.

- **No Declared Major** targets students who have not yet declared an academic major after the first year. The agent places an outbound call or sends a message to guide the student through program exploration and declaration. If the student engages, they are walked through available departments and programs conversationally, and the declaration is saved directly from the interaction, with no staff involvement required.
- **Freshman Orientation** contacts first-year students who need help navigating course selection early in their academic journey. The agent help them identify and enroll in the right courses for the semester, directly through the conversation, without the student needing to access any portal or contact the registrar.
- **Student Counselling** proactively reaches students who may need academic support, ensuring timely intervention before disengagement occurs. The agent conducts a structured check-in, captures the student's current academic challenges, course progress, low grades and barriers, then guides them toward booking a counselling appointment with their assigned academic advisor. Available timeslots are retrieved in real time from the advisor assigned to the student's department, presented conversationally, and the appointment is saved automatically upon confirmation.
- **Counselling Follow Up** is triggered when a student was previously contacted but remained hesitant about booking a counselling appointment, this campaign automatically re-engages them at a later point. The agent revisits the student's academic situation and previous conversation, addresses any concerns, and makes a renewed attempt to schedule the appointment.

Click a campaign to view its related information. A modal appears which contains a few tabs, each providing you with specific details.

<a id="General"></a>

## General

Displays the basic information of the campaign: name, description, type, status, and start/end dates.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/Outbound%20Student%20Engagement/Outbound%20Student%20Engagement.png)

<a id="CampaignDetails"></a>

## Campaign Details

Defines the campaign's outreach configuration, including the channel (Voice, SMS, or Web), the target audience, and the opening message delivered to the student at the start of the interaction.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/Outbound%20Student%20Engagement/Outbound%20Student%20Engagement_1.png)

The message supports dynamic fields such as **#PreferredFirstName** to personalize the outreach automatically.

> **Info:**  This is the first message the AI Agent sends to the client when the campaign starts.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/Outbound%20Student%20Engagement/Outbound%20Student%20Engagement_2.png)

<a id="RunHistory"></a>

## Run History

Provides a complete log of all campaign activities, showing each individual outreach attempt along with its status (New, Delivered, Initiated, Completed, etc.), type, and timestamp. This gives campaign managers full visibility into campaign execution at the activity level.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/Outbound%20Student%20Engagement/Outbound%20Student%20Engagement_3.png)

<a id="Engagement"></a>

## Engagement

Displays a visual summary of campaign performance, showing activities broken down by status. This allows campaign managers to quickly assess outreach outcomes and conversion rates at a glance.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/Outbound%20Student%20Engagement/Outbound%20Student%20Engagement_4.png)

At the end of the conversation, all interaction details—including any student counselling appointments—automatically save to the student record. You can view this in the **Students** view > **Campaign Student Details** form > **Campaign Interactions & Activities** tab.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/Outbound%20Student%20Engagement/Outbound%20Student%20Engagement_5.png)
