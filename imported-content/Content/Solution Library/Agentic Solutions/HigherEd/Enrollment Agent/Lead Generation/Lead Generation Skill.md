# Lead Generation Skill

The **Lead Generation Skill** helps higher education institutions shift from reactive lead handling to proactive engagement. By automating personalized outbound outreach at the top of the enrollment funnel, it enables admissions teams to qualify prospective student interest at scale without increasing headcount.

At the core of the solution is an outbound voice campaign that automatically contacts leads who have expressed interest in the institution. Using LLM-driven voice intelligence, the agent conducts natural, human-free phone conversations. During each call, it introduces the university, verifies lead identity, asks targeted discovery questions (such as intended start term and study interests), answers student questions, and schedules an appointment with an advisor. All responses save automatically to the interaction record for clear visibility into candidate fit.

The solution features two dedicated workspaces:

- **Campaign & Contacts**. Serves business users and campaign managers by centralizing campaigns, contacts, audiences, survey responses, activities, and message templates to build, execute, and track outreach.
- **Lead Generation Admin**. Serves administrators by providing control over reference data and operational settings, including campaign types, activity statuses, channel configurations, language codes, and audience filters.
