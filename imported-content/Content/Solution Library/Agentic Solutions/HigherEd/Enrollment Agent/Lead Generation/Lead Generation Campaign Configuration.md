# Lead Generation Campaign Configuration

Campaigns are created and managed within the **Campaign** section of the **Campaign & Contacts** workspace. The solution includes a pre-configured, out-of-the-box campaign —**Lead Generation** — designed for immediate deployment to target prospective student engagement scenarios.

Each campaign record contains the following tabs:

* **General**. Displays basic campaign information, including name, description, type, status, and scheduled start/end dates.

![](<../../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Lead Generation Campaign Configuration.png>)

* **Campaign Details**. Configures outreach parameters such as channel, target audience, and the initial message sent to the lead at the start of the interaction.

![](<../../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Lead Generation Campaign Configuration_1_1909x863.png>)

Dynamic fields (for example, #PreferredFirstName, #BotName, #UniversityName) automatically personalize initial messages.

![](<../../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Lead Generation Campaign Configuration_2.png>)

* **Survey Script**. Outlines the discovery questions and single-select response options used during prospective student outreach to capture candidate information.

![](<../../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Lead Generation Campaign Configuration_3.png>)

* **Survey Responses**. Captures all initiated lead surveys, detailing completion status, responses, duration, and start/end timestamps.

![](<../../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Lead Generation Campaign Configuration_4.png>)

* **Run History**. Tracks outreach activity execution at a granular level, logging attempt status (e.g., New, Delivered, Initiated, Completed), type, and precise timestamps.

![](<../../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Lead Generation Campaign Configuration_5.png>)

* **Engagement.** Offers a visual snapshot of campaign performance, breaking down activity by status to help campaign managers assess outreach efficiency and conversion rates at a glance.

![](<../../../../../../.gitbook/assets/Lead Generation Campaign Configuration_6.png>)
