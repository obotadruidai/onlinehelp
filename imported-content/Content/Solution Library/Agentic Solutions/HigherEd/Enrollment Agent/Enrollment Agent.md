# Enrollment Agent

The **Enrollment Agent** is an AI Agent designed to help higher education institutions streamline admission processes and simplify student admission. By automating key interactions, it enables admissions teams to scale outreach without increasing headcount.

<a id="Skills"></a>

## Skills

- **Lead Generation Skill** — Transforms lead management from reactive handling to proactive engagement. It automates personalized outbound outreach at the top of the enrollment funnel, allowing teams to evaluate and qualify prospective student interest at scale.
