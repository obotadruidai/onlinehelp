<a id="PatientEngagementCampaigns"></a>


for campaign managers

The Campaigns section in the **Campaign & Contacts** workspace contains all outbound campaigns. The solution includes three campaigns that are configured and ready to run out of the box.

- No Show campaign
- Balance Outreach campaign
- Demographic Verification campaign

Click a campaign to view its related information. A modal appears which contains a few tabs, each providing you with specific details.

<a id="General"></a>

## General

Displays the basic information of the campaign: name, description, type, status, and start/end dates.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/Outbound%20Patient%20Engagement/Outbound%20Patient%20Engagement_6.png)

<a id="CampaignDetails"></a>

## Campaign Details

Defines the campaign's outreach configuration, including the channel (Voice, SMS, or Web), the target audience, and the opening message delivered to the student at the start of the interaction.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/Outbound%20Patient%20Engagement/Outbound%20Patient%20Engagement_7.png)

The message supports dynamic fields such as **#PreferredFirstName** to personalize the outreach automatically.

> **Info:** This is the first message the AI Agent sends to the client when the campaign starts.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/Outbound%20Patient%20Engagement/Outbound%20Patient%20Engagement_8.png)

<a id="RunHistory"></a>

## Run History

Provides a complete log of all campaign activities, showing each individual outreach attempt along with its status (New, Delivered, Initiated, Completed, etc.), type, and timestamp. This gives campaign managers full visibility into campaign execution at the activity level.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/Outbound%20Patient%20Engagement/Outbound%20Patient%20Engagement_9.png)

<a id="Engagement"></a>

## Engagement

Displays a visual summary of campaign performance, showing activities broken down by status. This allows campaign managers to quickly assess outreach outcomes and conversion rates at a glance.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/Outbound%20Patient%20Engagement/Outbound%20Patient%20Engagement_10.png)

At the end of the conversation, all interaction details (including rescheduled appointments from a No Show campaign) are automatically saved to the patient record, which can be found under the **Appointment Management** workspace > **Account** form > **Activities** tab.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/Outbound%20Patient%20Engagement/Outbound%20Patient%20Engagement_11.png)
