The Healthcare AI Agents provide an end-to-end conversational AI framework tailored for healthcare providers, clinics, and payer organizations. They coordinate patient interactions across the full care journey — from initial appointment scheduling to post-discharge follow-up — reducing administrative burden on staff while improving the patient experience.
