<a id="PatientBillingAgent"></a>


The **Patient Billing Agent** is an intelligent financial orchestration layer built on top of the Healthcare Agentic Solution. It automates the complete post-appointment financial workflow, transforming medical billing into a seamless, conversational, and patient-friendly experience. By combining automated invoice generation, flexible payment management, and proactive communication, the solution reduces administrative overhead while improving payment collection efficiency and patient satisfaction.

The AI Agent is triggered automatically after the completion of an appointment and manages the entire payment lifecycle, from payment order generation to installment tracking and invoice settlement.

<a id="KeyCapabilities"></a>

## Key Capabilities

- **Complete Billing lifecycle** - Manages the full billing process, including payment order generation, invoice creation, installment management, payment tracking, and invoice settlement.
- **Dynamic Payment Flows** - Allows patients to either complete the payment in full or choose a flexible installment-based payment plan.
- **Installment Plan Automation** - Generates installment schedules and corresponding invoices automatically, including due dates and payment tracking.
- **Automated Email Notifications**- Sends payment confirmation emails, invoice details, installment schedules, and payment reminders automatically.
- **Multilingual Support**. The AI Agent detects and switches to any language instantly, ensuring a comfortable experience for everyone.
- **Billing Reporting**. The Patient Billing Agent features an integrated Billing reporting dashboard that provides real-time financial analytics across open balances, invoiced amounts, received payments, and upcoming subscription installments.

<a id="KeyComponents"></a>

## Key Components

The main components of the solution include:

- Business Workflows:

- **Appointment Completion Automation** – Automatically triggers the billing process after an appointment is completed by generating the corresponding payment order.
- **Billing Lifecycle Management** – Handles the complete billing flow, including invoice generation, payment processing, installment plan management, and payment status tracking.
- **Automated Alerts & Notifications** – Manages automated reminders, invoice delivery, installment notifications, and billing status updates.

- Two dedicated workspaces:

- **Billing Admin** workspace – Used by admins to configure payment methods, payment plans, and other variables.
- **Billing Management** workspace – A provider-focused view used to manage invoices, payment plans, payment orders, and payment installments.

- **Data Model**. Contains all essential billing entities (Payment Orders, Payment Plans, Invoices, Installments and Payments).
