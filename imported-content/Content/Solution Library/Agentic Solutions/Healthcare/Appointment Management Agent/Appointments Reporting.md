<a id="AppointmentsReporting"></a>


For medical staff

The Appointment Reporting section provides medical staff with real-time visibility into appointment activity and account data. It consists of two dashboards — Appointments and Accounts — each offering a set of charts that help track operational performance, monitor patient acquisition channels, and evaluate scheduling trends.

<a id="AppointmentsDashboard"></a>

## Appointments Dashboard

The Appointments dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of all scheduled appointments within a selected date range. It includes six charts that break down appointment data by status, date, time of day, medical speciality, facility, and booking source. Together, these charts help clinical and administrative teams monitor workload distribution, identify scheduling patterns, and assess the effectiveness of different booking channels.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/appointment%20management%20agent/appt%20dashboard.png)

> **Info:** Any chart segment or slice is interactive — clicking on it drills down to the list of records that match the selected item.

<a id="ByStatusChart"></a>

### By Status Chart

The chart gives staff an instant snapshot of how appointments are spread across all lifecycle stages, making it easy to identify backlogs, track cancellation rates, or monitor how many visits are still pending confirmation or action.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/appointment%20management%20agent/by%20status.png)

This pie chart displays the current distribution of appointments grouped by their status. Each slice represents one of the following status categories:

- **New** — A scheduled appointment that has been booked and is awaiting action.
- **Confirmed** — An appointment that has been verified by the patient or staff.
- **Canceled** — An appointment that was canceled and will not take place.
- **Rescheduled** — An appointment that was moved to a new date or time slot.
- **Completed** — A visit that has been successfully finalized.
- **No Show** — An appointment where the patient did not attend and the status was automatically assigned by the system.

<a id="ByDateAndStatusChart"></a>

### By Date And Status Chart

This bar breaks down appointments per day counts over time, with each bar broken down by status. Each bar on the horizontal axis represents a specific date within the selected period, making it easy to see daily appointment volumes and how status types are distributed across different days. This view helps track day-to-day fluctuations and identify specific dates that experienced higher volumes of new, completed, rescheduled or canceled appointments.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/appointment%20management%20agent/date%20and%20status.png)

<a id="ByDateAndTimeOfDayChart"></a>

### By Date And Time Of Day Chart

This stacked bar chart shows appointment volumes per date, segmented by the part of the day in which they are scheduled:

- **Morning** — Appointments taking place in the early hours of the day.
- **Noon** — Appointments scheduled around midday.
- **Afternoon** — Appointments falling in the mid-to-late daytime hours.
- **Evening** — Appointments booked for the later part of the day.
- **Night** — Appointments scheduled during night hours.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/appointment%20management%20agent/date%20and%20time.png)

By combining date and time-of-day dimensions in a single view, the chart reveals not only how busy each day is, but also which parts of the day consistently attract the most bookings. This helps administrators fine-tune slot availability, align staffing levels with peak demand periods, and ensure that staff capacity is distributed effectively throughout both the day and the week.

<a id="BySpecialityChart"></a>

### By Speciality Chart

This bar chart tracks appointment volume relative to specific medical fields (e.g., General Practice, Trauma Orthopedics, Clinical Cardiology). It highlights which departments carry the highest patient load and which have lower activity, supporting resource planning, capacity management, and decisions around speciality-specific staffing.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/appointment%20management%20agent/by%20specialty.png)

<a id="ByFacilityChart"></a>

### By Facility Chart

This bar chart illustrates the volume of appointments processed across different physical locations or clinic facilities (e.g., Cardiology Wing A, GM Clinic 1, Lakeside Medical).

![](../../../../Resources/Images/appointment%20management%20agent/facility.png)

It allows medical staff to compare activity levels across locations, identify facilities that are over- or under-utilized, and make data-driven decisions about resource allocation or capacity adjustments at specific locations.

<a id="BySourceChart"></a>

### By Source Chart

This bar chart tracks how appointments were booked, categorized by the channel through which the patient initiated the request. The available sources are:

- **Website** — Appointments booked directly through the online patient portal or website form.
- **Voice AI Agent** — Appointments scheduled through a voice-based AI Agent that interacts with patients over the phone.
- **Call Center** — Appointments booked by a human agent during a phone call with the patient.
- **Web AI Agent** — Appointments scheduled through conversations patients had with the AI Agent deployed on the web channel (e.g., a chat widget on the website).
- **Referral** — Appointments originated from a referral by another healthcare provider or partner.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/appointment%20management%20agent/channel.png)

This chart is valuable for evaluating the effectiveness of each booking channel and informing decisions about where to focus patient engagement or automation investments.

<a id="AccountsDashboard"></a>

## Accounts Dashboard

The Accounts dashboard focuses on user profiling (patient and prospects accounts) and engagement origination within the selected date range. It helps medical providers understand who is interacting with their systems and which digital channels are most effective at attracting new account registrations.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/appointment%20management%20agent/accounts%20dash.png)

<a id="ByTypeChart"></a>

### By Type Chart

This chart shows the breakdown of accounts by classification. Monitoring this ratio helps the medical team understand how many new potential patients are entering the system compared to returning ones — useful for tracking outreach and conversion effectiveness.

- **Patient** — An individual who has already engaged with the healthcare provider and has an established record.
- **Prospect** — An individual who has not yet become an active patient; for example, someone who initiated a booking inquiry or was registered through a digital channel but has not yet completed a visit.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/appointment%20management%20agent/accounts%20by%20type.png)

<a id="BySourceChart1"></a>

### By Source Chart

This chart shows the breakdown of accounts by the channel through which they were acquired or registered. Monitoring this distribution helps the medical team identify which intake channels are driving the most accounts:

- **Website** — An account created through the provider's website, typically via an online form or self-registration flow.
- **Call Center** — An account created as a result of an inbound or outbound call handled by a call center agent.
- **Referral** — An account originating from a referral, either from another patient, a partner, or an external healthcare provider.
- **Web AI Agent** — An account initiated through an AI Agent deployed on the web channel (e.g., a chat widget on the website).
- **Voice AI Agent** — An account initiated through a voice-based AI Agent that interacts with people over the phone.

![](../../../../Resources/Images/appointment%20management%20agent/accounts%20source.png)–
