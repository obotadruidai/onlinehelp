<a id="ConfiguringSolutionTemplates"></a>


To configure the solution template, follow these steps:

1. [Set up the AI Agent](Set%20up%20the%20bot.md).
2. [Train entities and the AI Agent](Train_entities_and_bot.md).
3. [Configure webchat](Set_up_webchat_in_html_page.md).
4. If you want to use the UiPath AI Center for OCR of identity cards which comply with the MRZ standard, you also need to [set up UiPath AI Center](Setting_up_AI_Center.md).
5. [Download and set up the UiPath processes](Setting_up_UiPath_Processes.md).
