## Open a new ticket

If you want to open a new ticket, just type “I want to add a ticket”

![](../../../Resources/Images/See%20Ticket%20Management%20in%20action/See%20Ticket%20Management%20in%20action.png)

The AI Agent will guide you through the ticket opening process:

![](../../../Resources/Images/See%20Ticket%20Management%20in%20action/See%20Ticket%20Management%20in%20action_1.png)

![](../../../Resources/Images/See%20Ticket%20Management%20in%20action/See%20Ticket%20Management%20in%20action_2.png)

After collecting all the information, an unattended UiPath process is triggered and the ticket will be registered in ServiceNow App.

![](../../../Resources/Images/See%20Ticket%20Management%20in%20action/See%20Ticket%20Management%20in%20action_3.png)

Please see below the ticket registered in ServiceNow:

![](../../../Resources/Images/See%20Ticket%20Management%20in%20action/See%20Ticket%20Management%20in%20action_4.png)

<a id="Addcommentphotototheticket"></a>

## Add comment/photo to the ticket

Once you have opened a ticket, you can just type “my tickets” and you will have access to:

- see the status of all your tickets.
- add photos/comments on any opened ticket.

![](../../../Resources/Images/See%20Ticket%20Management%20in%20action/See%20Ticket%20Management%20in%20action_5.png)
