## My cards

The AI Agent will guide the customer through multiple self-service operations, such as:

- block a card
- unblock a card
- issue a new card
- change withdrawal limit
- unblock a PIN code

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how.png)

<a id="Blockacard"></a>

### Block a card

If you need to block a specific card, just type “I want to block a card”:

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_1.png)

The AI Agent shows you all your available cards:

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_2.png)

Choose the card you want to be blocked:

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_3.png)

The AI Agent performs the operation in the systems and confirms you that the card was blocked:

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_4.png)

<a id="Unblockacard"></a>

### Unblock a card

If you want to unblock a specific card, just type “I want to unblock a card”:

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_5.png)

The AI Agent displays all your cards that are blocked in the system:

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_6.png)

Choose the card you want to unblock.

The AI Agent performs the operation in the systems and confirms you that the card was blocked:

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_7.png)

<a id="Issueanewcard"></a>

### Issue a new card

If you need a new card, just type “I need a new card”

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_8.png)

The AI Agent asks you more information related to the issuance of the new card:

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_9.png)

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_10.png)

An “Card application Form” is issued automatically and you will be asked to sign it.

In order to sign, choose “I agree”.

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_11.png)

eSignature Namirial is a solution that uses Namirial technology to provide your AI Agent with electronic signature capabilities. In the screen below, type “1234”

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_12.png)

Sign the document and click on “Finish”:

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_13.png)

After you sign the document, the AI Agent informs you that the card will be issued and it will be delivered to your home address.

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_14.png)

<a id="Changewithdrawallimit"></a>

### Change withdrawal limit

If you want to change the withdrawal limit of your card, just type “Change card limit”.

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_15.png)

Choose the card on which you want to perform this change.

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_16.png)

Enter the new limit.

The AI Agent performs the action in the system and informs you that the limit was changed.

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_17.png)

<a id="UnblockaPINcode"></a>

### Unblock a PIN code

If you blocked your PIN and you want to unblock it, just type “Unblock a PIN code”.

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_18.png)

Choose the card for which you want to unlock the PIN code.

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_19.png)

The AI Agent performs the action in the system and informs you that the PIN was unblock.

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_20.png)

<a id="Contracts"></a>

## Contracts

As a customer, you can perform the following actions on your contracts:

- View details about your contract
- Search for a contract
- Modify personal information
- Terminate a contract

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_21.png)

If you want to find a specific contract just type “My contracts” and choose the contract. Click on “Details” and the AI Agent displays all the information related to your contract.

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_22.png)

If you want to update a specific information on the contract, click on “Update contract”.

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_23.png)

Choose the information you want to update.

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_24.png)

In order to update your information, the AI Agent generates an “personal information update form”  and asks you to sign it.

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_25.png)

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_26.png)

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_27.png)

As soon as the form is signed, the information will be updated in the system.

<a id="Payments"></a>

## Payments

As a customer, you can perform the following actions related to your payments:

- Apply for payment suspension due to COVID-19 situation
- Confirm a payment
- Perform a fund transfer
- Check the exchange rate\*

\*The exchange rate conversational workflow is integrated with an UiPath unattended robot.

![](../../../Resources/Images/Test%20Self-Service%20Financial%20Operations/This%20section%20describes%20how_28.png)
