# Test Self-Service Financial Operations

### My cards

The AI Agent will guide the customer through multiple self-service operations, such as:

* block a card
* unblock a card
* issue a new card
* change withdrawal limit
* unblock a PIN code

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how.png>)

#### Block a card

If you need to block a specific card, just type “I want to block a card”:

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_1.png>)

The AI Agent shows you all your available cards:

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_2.png>)

Choose the card you want to be blocked:

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_3.png>)

The AI Agent performs the operation in the systems and confirms you that the card was blocked:

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_4.png>)

#### Unblock a card

If you want to unblock a specific card, just type “I want to unblock a card”:

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_5.png>)

The AI Agent displays all your cards that are blocked in the system:

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_6.png>)

Choose the card you want to unblock.

The AI Agent performs the operation in the systems and confirms you that the card was blocked:

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_7.png>)

#### Issue a new card

If you need a new card, just type “I need a new card”

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_8.png>)

The AI Agent asks you more information related to the issuance of the new card:

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_9.png>)

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_10.png>)

An “Card application Form” is issued automatically and you will be asked to sign it.

In order to sign, choose “I agree”.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_11.png>)

eSignature Namirial is a solution that uses Namirial technology to provide your AI Agent with electronic signature capabilities. In the screen below, type “1234”

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_12.png>)

Sign the document and click on “Finish”:

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_13.png>)

After you sign the document, the AI Agent informs you that the card will be issued and it will be delivered to your home address.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_14.png>)

#### Change withdrawal limit

If you want to change the withdrawal limit of your card, just type “Change card limit”.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_15.png>)

Choose the card on which you want to perform this change.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_16.png>)

Enter the new limit.

The AI Agent performs the action in the system and informs you that the limit was changed.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_17.png>)

#### Unblock a PIN code

If you blocked your PIN and you want to unblock it, just type “Unblock a PIN code”.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_18.png>)

Choose the card for which you want to unlock the PIN code.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_19.png>)

The AI Agent performs the action in the system and informs you that the PIN was unblock.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_20.png>)

### Contracts

As a customer, you can perform the following actions on your contracts:

* View details about your contract
* Search for a contract
* Modify personal information
* Terminate a contract

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_21.png>)

If you want to find a specific contract just type “My contracts” and choose the contract. Click on “Details” and the AI Agent displays all the information related to your contract.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_22.png>)

If you want to update a specific information on the contract, click on “Update contract”.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_23.png>)

Choose the information you want to update.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_24.png>)

In order to update your information, the AI Agent generates an “personal information update form”  and asks you to sign it.

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_25.png>)

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_26.png>)

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/This section describes how_27.png>)

As soon as the form is signed, the information will be updated in the system.

### Payments

As a customer, you can perform the following actions related to your payments:

* Apply for payment suspension due to COVID-19 situation
* Confirm a payment
* Perform a fund transfer
* Check the exchange rate\*

\*The exchange rate conversational workflow is integrated with an UiPath unattended robot.
