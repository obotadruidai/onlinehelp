---

<a id="SmallTalk"></a>


This solution template enables you to provide your AI Agent with predefined messages, including:

- greetings (e.g. good morning, hello, etc)
- about the AI Agent ( are you a robot, how old are you, do you have a girlfriend...)
- interaction (tell me a story, tell me a joke)
- courtesy (you are doing great, awesome job).

This solution template comes with two languages: Romanian (RO) and English (EN-US). It contains no integrations.
