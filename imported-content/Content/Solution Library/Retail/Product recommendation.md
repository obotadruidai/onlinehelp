# Product Recommendation

This solution template enables you to bring your products closer to your audience! It comes with conversational flows that will guide the website visitor through the shopping process. The AI Agent understands the user's intent and displays a range of product options based on the visitor’s preferences.

Customers can choose the product they want to purchase, add the products to the shopping cart and place their order via the AI Agent. You can also design the AI Agent to provide information about upcoming products relevant to the customer’s intent and allow them to pre-order new products once they start being available.

Recommendation engines integrated to this AI Agent can help retailers increase the sales and help users discover products that fit very well with their tastes and requirements.

### Configure the template

After you import this solution, configure it following the procedure described in [Configuring Skill Templates](<General Settings/Configuring Solution Templates.md>).

### See the solution in action

In the chat, type “I want to buy a gift”.

The AI Agent understands the intent and offers some suggestions.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Product recommendation.png>)

Type “no” and the AI Agent will search for another recommendation asking some additional questions to filter the results.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Product recommendation_1.png>)

The AI Agent will search in the database and it will show the available products.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Product recommendation_2.png>)

Click “Add to cart”.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Product recommendation_3.png>)

To complete the order, click the “My cart” button.

### My shopping cart

This skill provides the conversational flow to display the customer's shopping cart summary, apply vouchers, order and pay.

#### See the skill in action

In the chat, type “My shopping cart”

The AI Agent shows your shopping cart summary.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Product recommendation_4.png>)

Click "Use voucher".

Click “Apply voucher”.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Product recommendation_5.png>)

Choose the "Courier" delivery method.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Product recommendation_6.png>)

Confirm the delivery address.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Product recommendation_7.png>)

The order summery is shown. Choose the payment method.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/Product recommendation_8.png>)

Choose either "Card on delivery" or "Cash on delivery".

The order is placed.
