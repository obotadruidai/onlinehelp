# Automotive

Conversational business applications today are transforming the automotive industry while creating a revolution in its real sense. There is a huge potential in the way the technology helps the automotive industry in dealing with customers, saving costs, generating leads or increasing customer engagement.

<a id="TopbenefitsofusingAIAgentsintheautomotiveindustry"></a>

## Top benefits of using AI Agents in the automotive industry

- **Always available**. With the new type of digital interactions, clients expect a business to be accessible and convenient. Car dealerships deal with many requests and complains every day while their workforce is not constantly available. This is where the AI Agents step in to make their service available 24/7 and provide the customers a positive first-hand experience.
- **Streamlined sales, delivery and post-sale experience**. While the sales and delivery of a car can be challenging, AI Agents can help establishing coordination between every part of the delivery chain which leads to greater client engagement and retention by the car dealership. They also can help clients track down the car delivery, schedule post-sale service appointments, and create a streamlined post-sales experience for the customer.
- **Generate more leads for a car dealership**. As in any other industry, the marketing and sales strategy of car dealerships depend on lead generation, leads with a high conversion rate. Integrated in various communication channels, AI Agents can act as efficient lead generators. AI Agent can use the data and insights collected from conversations to create substantial leads for a car dealership. They interact directly with the interested leads and push them forward in the buyer’s journey.
