# Employee Self-Service

This solution template provides a wide range of skills for employees:

<a id="Generatecertificates"></a>

### Generate certificates

The HR AI Agent helps you generate different types of certificates with or without digital signature. Available certificates including: employee certificate (school/kindergarten child registration, etc), tax deduction, medical, bank certificate, COVID-19 certificates to prove different changes in the employee contract.

![](../../../Resources/Images/solution%20templates/Cristina/generate%20certificates.png)

<a id="RegisterCancelRescheduleLeaveRequest"></a>

### Register/Cancel/Reschedule Leave Request

The HR AI Agent helps you keep track of your days off balance. Basis on the calculation performed, you can register a new leave request like (Sick leave, Maternity leave, Anniversary leave, Unpaid leave, Holiday leave, Quarantine leave, Paternal leave). After the registration is done, the leave is request is saved in the HR system and your manager will get a notification. Also, if you’ve changed your mind, you can even cancel or reschedule your leave request.

![](../../../Resources/Images/solution%20templates/Cristina/leave%20request.png)

<a id="Timesheet"></a>

### Timesheet

Enables you to register your arrival time/leaving time, or you can check in working hours for your existing projects. At the end of the registration, your changes will be updated in the HR system.

![](../../../Resources/Images/solution%20templates/Cristina/timesheet.png)

<a id="FeedbackConversationTemplate"></a>

### Feedback Conversation Template

Conversation templates available in the HR AI Agent to capture feedback from your employees, such as employee satisfaction surveys, targeted surveys (team building location, professional development courses, personal development courses: acting classes, and so on).

<a id="FAQ"></a>

### FAQ

Different types of questions about procedures, policies, security, rules and regulations, benefits and compensations, specific types of leave requests and documents associated with them (maternity, sick leave, etc.). It also provides FAQs specific to the pandemic: communication guide, working-from-home (WFH) procedures and regulations, teleworking guidance, and support.

<a id="Account360"></a>

### Account 360

Get 360 information regarding your account. Integrated with the HR systems and based on your role the AI Agent will offer you the appropriate information regarding your account, such as:

| User | Account Information |
| --- | --- |
| Manager | My tasks, leave requests waiting for approval, leave requests already approved, add a new manager in the system. |
| Employee | Onboarding after employment, Register new leave request (Sick leave, Maternity leave, Anniversary leave, Unpaid leave, Holiday leave, Quarantine leave, Paternal leave), Get the status of my available days, Cancel a leave request, Ask for a new certificate (employee, deduction, medical), Update employee personal data, FAQ regarding internal systems, Get the list of compensation and benefits that you are provided with, or the specific list of trainings that you need to perform in order to develop your career. |
| Candidate | Get the list of all available jobs, apply for a new job position, upload your cv, alerts and notifications when your interview was scheduled. |

<a id="Payroll"></a>

### Payroll

The AI Agent provides for you as an Employee your earnings statement, including bonuses and compensations. Also, you can perform the T&E management with actions like register a new T&E or register request for new money in advance. Based on the data read by the AI Agent you will receive your earning statement report and the HR admin will have a payroll overview for each employee.
