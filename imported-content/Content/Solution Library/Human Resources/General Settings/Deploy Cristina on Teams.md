# Deploy Cristina on Teams

You can deploy your Human Resources solution on Teams by following the instructions described in section [MS Teams Integration](../../../Channels/MS%20Teams/MS%20Teams%20Integration.md).

To deploy Human Resources on Teams for personal use, download [Cristina-Teams.zip](https://druiddocs.file.core.windows.net/docs-shares/CristinaHR/Cristina-Teams.zip?st=2021-02-14T07%3A40%3A46Z&se=2021-12-31T07%3A40%3A00Z&sp=rl&sv=2018-03-28&sr=f&sig=T7lX2QHv%2FDGOqGfj%2B7Uxvwe2FG0GfY%2FReaS05ZEpKmU%3D), then follow the instructions described in section [MS Teams Integration](../../../Channels/MS%20Teams/MS%20Teams%20Integration.md).
