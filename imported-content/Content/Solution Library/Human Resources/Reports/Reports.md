# Reports

This solution template allows you to generate advanced reports in PDF, MS Word or Excel format by simply asking the AI Agent, eliminating the tedious tasks of searching for information in various enterprise repositories and applications. Fuel faster and better business decisions with on-the-fly actionable insights and gain unmatched time-to-market that will put your company one step ahead the competition.

As an HR Admin/Manager, you can track the following actions:

- Check channels for candidate applications (AI Agent, Facebook, LinkedIn, etc.).
- Get the list with the candidates attending or missing an interview.
- Get the list of accepted candidates, drill-down on position and department.
- Get the list of rejected candidates, drill-down on position and department.
- Get the list with candidates transferred to another manager for onboarding.
- Get the list of employees with onboarding via AI Agent during the last month or a specific time frame.
- Get the list of employees coming from unemployment.
- Get the list of employees with disabilities.
- Get the list of candidates who received the employment offer via AI Agent.
- How many candidates have signed the employment offer.
- What jobs openings were filled in the last month with drill-down on each department.
- Get number of certificates with drill-down per each certificate.
- Get number of leave requests with drill down on each leave request type and employee.
- Who are the employees with no sick leave during a selected time frame.
- Reports on how many employees have updated their personal data in the system. Which employees still need to update their data.
- How many hours did the employee X worked on project Y (timesheet registration flow).
- Get monthly working hours for each employee.
- Get the list of employees with a completed evaluation.
- Get the list of employees that received bonus/salary increase or job promotion in a specific time.
- Get the list of employees with off-boarding via AI Agent.
- Get the list of employees that applied for a training.
- Get the list of employees that were quarantined or returned to work in a specific period.
- How many employees are working in the office today/this week.

You can personalize the reports and based on your configuration, you can add other relevant KPIs.
