<a id="Recruitment"></a>


This solution template provides onboarding skills for HR Admins and new employes, as follows:

<a id="Recruitmentpositionapplication"></a>

### Recruitment position application

As a possible Candidate, you are going to have access to all available job openings within the company. You will be able to apply via AI Agent on the desired job position, receive the job description, upload your CV, schedule your interview, and receive reminders regarding your interview.

![](../../../Resources/Images/solution%20templates/Cristina/application.png)

<a id="Offboarding"></a>

### Off-boarding

For the exit interview, all employees will have access to the set of exit interview questions or surveys. Conversationally, all the details will be filled in the HR system and on the employee’s digital file. Please note that this part is specific to each customer methodology of exit interviews.

<a id="RecruitmentProcessManagement"></a>

### Recruitment Process Management

As an HR Admin/Manager, you are going to have access to conversations that will let you open new job positions within the company. After the position is created and all the details associated with the job are filled in, the candidates will automatically have access to the latest available job positions. You will receive your alerts and notifications every time a new candidate applies on your job. You can schedule your interview, accept, reject, or add the candidates to favorites.

After the new position is created, using RPA and the AI Agent connector the job will be automatically posted on all the advertising channels (both internally and external).

![](../../../Resources/Images/solution%20templates/Cristina/recruitment.png)
