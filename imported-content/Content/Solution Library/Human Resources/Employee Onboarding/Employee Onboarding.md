# Employee Onboarding

This solution template provides onboarding skills for HR Admins and new employes, as follows:

<a id="Onboardingbeforeemployment"></a>

### Onboarding before employment

As an HR Admin you are going to have access to the onboarding new employee workflow including pre-screening questions, offer generator and digital contract signature, creating new employee account automatically in the HR system or using the OCR of the identity card and childbirth certificate to validate the identity of the employee and save the documents on each employee digital file.

![](../../../Resources/Images/solution%20templates/Cristina/preemployment.png)

<a id="Onboardingafteremployment"></a>

### Onboarding after employment

On your first day at work the AI Agent can guide you through the work and safety program or COVID-19 regulations inside the company and you can even register your request to work from home. Also, the AI Agent will guide you through the basic steps of your first technology setup guide (VPN access, obtain passwords and access to various folders depending on your position, configure badge access registration, etc.).

As part of your first day at work, the AI Agent will help you choose your non-financial benefits like gym or movie subscription.

The AI Agent will send you notifications and different reminders to bring documents to your employer such as diplomas, awards, course graduation, fill in surveys or complete your tasks.

![](../../../Resources/Images/solution%20templates/Cristina/afteremployment.png)
