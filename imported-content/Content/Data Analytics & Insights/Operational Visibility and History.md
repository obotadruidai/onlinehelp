# Operational Visibility and History

Druid provides real-time and historical transparency into every session to support both human teams and automated workflows. By centralizing interaction data, the platform ensures that administrators and supervisors have the necessary oversight to monitor AI Agent execution and manage seamless transitions to human support staff.

<a id="AIAgentSummaryDashboard"></a>

## AI Agent Summary Dashboard

Druid offers the AI Agent Summary Dashboard to provide standardized tracking for engagement rates and automation success. This interface serves as the primary high-level view for monitoring the health and adoption of the conversational ecosystem.

- Engagement Metrics: Track total users, session counts, and message volumes to understand peak usage periods and resource requirements.
- Automation Rates: Measure the percentage of queries resolved by the AI Agent versus those requiring escalation, providing a clear baseline for automation efficiency.
- Channel Distribution: Visualize how users interact across different platforms—such as Webchat, WhatsApp, or MS Teams—to ensure a consistent experience across the entire AI Agent orchestration layer.

<a id="ConversationHistory"></a>

## Conversation History

Druid provides Conversation History for advanced interaction drill-down, supporting deep-dive analysis and auditing. This interface allows for the forensic review of individual sessions to verify AI Agent accuracy and user intent.

- **Full-Text Search**: Rapidly locate specific interactions using keywords, user identifiers, or specific metadata tags.
- **Context Filtering**: Narrow down history by date range, channel, specific AI Agent, or conversation outcome to identify trends or recurring issues.
- **Session Playback**: Review the complete Conversation Transcript and Conversation Trace to see exactly how the dialogue evolved, including the background logic steps performed by the system.

<a id="LiveAIGuidanceforHumanTeams"></a>

## Live AI Guidance for Human Teams

Druid offers Live AI Guidance to provide real-time visibility into active sessions, assisting human agents during handoffs. This ensures that the transition from automation to human intervention is informed and efficient.

- **Active Session Monitoring**: Agents can monitor live interactions as they occur, allowing for proactive intervention if a conversation requires human expertise.
- **Contextual Handoffs**: When an AI Agent escalates a session, the human agent receives the full conversation context, including previous intents matched and data gathered, eliminating the need for the user to repeat information.
- **AI Agent Assist**: During the live handoff, the platform can use an Assistant AI Agent to suggest relevant Knowledge Base articles or responses to the human agent, accelerating the resolution rate for complex queries.
