# Take Snapshots

In the Solution configuration page, click the Solution Snapshots section header. The section expands. At the top-right corner of the table, click the **Take Snapshot** button.

![](../Resources/Images/Working%20with%20solutions/Take%20Snapshots.png)

DRUID generates the solution configuration archive (zip file) and saves it in the Druid Portal. The solution snapshot appears in the table.

![](../Resources/Images/Working%20with%20solutions/Take%20Snapshots_1.png)

When importing a solution, a snapshot is automatically created and marked as a backup snapshot. When taking solution snapshots, they are not marked as backup snapshots (**Is Backup** is "false").
