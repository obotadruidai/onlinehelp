<a id="ElevenLabsVoices"></a>


You can explore and create ElevenLabs voices directly in the Druid Portal to easily manage your ElevenLabs speech resources.

For both ElevenLabs resources, STT and TTS, you can do the following in the Druid Portall after adding ElevenLabs resources:

- Explore the list of voices saved in your ElevenLabs account, customize existing profiles, and create brand new custom voices
- Configure voice parameters and behaviors based on preferences.
- Test voices and resource configurations before using them in speech resources.
- View speech resource usage.

> **Info:** ElevenLabs Voices are available starting with Druid 10.1.

<a id="AccessElevenLabsvoices"></a>

## Access ElevenLabs voices

To access and manage ElevenLabs voice options in your portal workspace:

1. Navigate to **Administration** > **Speech Resources**.
2. Locate your ElevenLabs resource and click the **Preview** icon in the Actions column.

![](../Resources/Images/ElevenLabs%20Voices/ElevenLabs%20Voices.png)

3. Use the top navigation tabs to switch between **Voices**, **Configurations**, **Playground**, and **Metrics**.

![](../Resources/Images/ElevenLabs%20Voices/ElevenLabs%20Voices_1.png)

When you preview ElevenLabs resources in the Druid Portal, the Voices displays by default with the **Explore** tab selected.

> **IMPORTANT!** ElevenLabs Voice Library requires a paid ElevenLabs account tier and is not available for free tier users.

![](../Resources/Images/ElevenLabs%20Voices/Voices%20Library.png)

The Voices page contains three primary tabs:

- **Explore**. Displays the complete public ElevenLabs Voices Library.
- **Created**. Lists all custom voices you have generated within the Druid Portal.
- **Saved**. Displays voices saved in your ElevenLabs account under My Voices, plus any voices saved from the **Explore** tab (in the Actions column).

> **Info:** Voices saved and created directly within the Druid Portal automatically synchronize and appear in your ElevenLabs account under My Voices.

<a id="Filteringvoices"></a>

## Filtering voices

You can browse and locate voices in the Voices Library using multiple methods:

- **Text Search**: Use the search bar to filter voices by name, description keyword, language, or gender.
- **Audio Sample Search**: Drag and drop or upload an audio file to search for original matching voices and similar acoustic profiles.

Click the **Play** icon in the Actions column to preview any voice before saving or using it.
