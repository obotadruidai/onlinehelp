title: "Customizing the dashboard with custom KPIs"
---

<a id="CustomizingthedashboardwithcustomKPIs"></a>


To customize the dashboard with your own KPIs (other than Druid predefined KPIs), get in touch with [Druid Tech Support](<mailto:support@druidai.com?subject=Dashboard Customization with custom KPIs>).

If you have custom time series KPIs in your dashboard, go to **Dashboard** > **Settings** and click the **Re-calculate** button and the **Refresh** button, otherwise the Dashboard will not register the custom KPIs and your chart and table widgets will show no data.

When the refresh status is “Completed”, Druid finished calculating the KPIs. For more information, see [Dashboard](AI%20Agent%20Summary%20Dashboard.md), section *Enabling Time Series KPIs*.
