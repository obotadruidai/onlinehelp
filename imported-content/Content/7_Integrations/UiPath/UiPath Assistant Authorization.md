---
title: "UiPath Assistant Authorization"
---

<a id="UiPathAssistantAuthorization"></a>


The first interaction between the browser and the UiPath Assistant will trigger an authorization process:

![https://www.druidai.com/wiki/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Pop-up.jpg](../../Resources/Images/Running%20UiPath%20Attended%20Processes/UiPath%20Assistant%20Authorization.jpg)

Click **Open UiPath Assistant**. The UiPath Assistant access request pop-up appears.

![https://www.druidai.com/wiki/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Authorization-2.jpg](../../Resources/Images/Running%20UiPath%20Attended%20Processes/UiPath%20Assistant%20Authorization_1.jpg)

Click **Allow**. After the authorization process is complete, the robot will start the attended process.

> **NOTE:** The UiPath authorization process happens is valid for 30 days, unless the browser is used in incognito mode.
