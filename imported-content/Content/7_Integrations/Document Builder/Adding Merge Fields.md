---
title: "Adding Merge Fields"
---

<a id="AddingMergeFields"></a>


To insert merge fields, follow these steps:

1. In the main document, click where you want to insert the field.
2. In the **Insert** tab, click **Quick Parts** and then **Field…**.

![](../../Resources/Images/Document%20Builder/Adding%20Merge%20Fields.png)

The Field page appears.

3. From the Field names, select **MergeField**.
4. In the **Field name**, enter the name of the entity if it is the start/end tag or the name of the entity field. Example:

![](../../Resources/Images/Document%20Builder/Adding%20Merge%20Fields_1.png)

5. Click **OK**.
