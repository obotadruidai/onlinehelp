# Edit Workspaces

> **NOTE:** Only users who have been granted a security role with permission **[Workspace edit]** can edit workspaces. For more information, see [Define Security Roles for Business Users](Define%20Security%20Roles%20for%20Business%20Users.md).

You can edit workspaces by changing their general details, by adding, editing or removing links from the menu. You can also change your workspace security.
