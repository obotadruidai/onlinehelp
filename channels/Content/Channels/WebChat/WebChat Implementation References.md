<a id="DruidCustomMade"></a>

## Druid Custom-Made

The following examples show how clients have implemented webchat using Druid custom styling and behavior.

<a id="GUS"></a>

### GUS

GUS is Georgia Southern University’s AI Agent, helping students and visitors get quick answers and support anytime.

<a id="Blaze"></a>

### Blaze

Blaze is Valdosta State University’s AI Agent, helping students and visitors quickly find information and support online.

<a id="Cody"></a>

### Cody

Cody is the digital assistant of Columbus State University, available 24/7 to answer questions and guide users to the right resources.

<a id="Fred"></a>

### Fred

Fred is the virtual real estate agent for Freder. It assists customers in their property search, helps them list their properties, and efficiently gathers query or issue details, offers troubleshooting support, and forwards the information to the appropriate teams for resolution.

<a id="Laila"></a>

### Laila

Laila is a commercial AI Agent that assists customers with inquiries and services, and is recognized as the first fully conversational AI assistant in the telecom industry.

### #ChatEU

ChatEurope's AI Agent helps people across the continent understand what's happening, from elections to climate policy, all in their own language, with trusted sources to back it up.

### Alice

Alice is the AI-powered travel assistant available on alicetipsandtrips.com dedicated to providing you with the most personalized travel web journeys.
