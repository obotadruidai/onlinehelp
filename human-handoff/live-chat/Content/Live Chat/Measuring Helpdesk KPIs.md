title: Measuring Helpdesk KPIs
---


Measuring the performance of your helpdesk team requires you to track specific KPIs.

The Druid Portal provides you with the possibility to create extensive KPI dashboards to help you monitor the customer service performance.

{% hint style="info" %}
**NOTE:** Customizations are required on the conversation history database to have additional KPIs listed in the Dashboard. For information on how to do it, get in touch with [DRUID Tech Support](../Live%20Chat/mailto:support@druidai.com) or your DRUID implementation partner.
{% endhint %}
