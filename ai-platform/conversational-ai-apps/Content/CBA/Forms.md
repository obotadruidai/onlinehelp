---
title: Forms
---


Forms are user interface elements that enable authors and business users to interact with individual entity records in views.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/form in view.png>)

Authors can create multiple forms for the same entity to accommodate different contexts in which business users interact with the entity. For example, some forms may allow users to add new records, others may only permit editing existing records, and some may be read-only. Forms can also include multiple tabs, with simple or complex sections that facilitate data manipulation, such as adding new record details or attaching files.
