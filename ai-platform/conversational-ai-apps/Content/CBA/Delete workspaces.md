---
title: Delete Workspaces
---


{% hint style="info" %}
**NOTE:** Only users who have been granted a security role with permission **\[Workspace delete]** can delete workspaces. For more information, see [Define Security Roles for Business Users](<Define Security Roles for Business Users.md>).
{% endhint %}

From the **All Settings** menu, click **Workspaces**. The list of work spaces to which you have access displays.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/WORKSPACES LIST.png>)

To remove a workspace, click the delete icon (![](<../../.gitbook/assets/delete icon.png>) ) displayed in line with the workspace. A confirmation dialog appears. Click **Yes**. The dialog closes and the workspace is deleted.
