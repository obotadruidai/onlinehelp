---
title: Preview and Validate Views
---


Authors can preview a view by clicking the **Preview** icon at the top of the View Diagram.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/preview icon.png>)

The preview opens in a new browser tab, allowing you to check how the view will appear to users.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/view preview.png>)

{% hint style="info" %}
**Info:** If no records exist at the time of preview or if integrations have not been configured, the preview will display with no data.
{% endhint %}

To refine the preview, authors can filter the view and display only the fields hidden on mobile devices by tapping **Fields on Mobile** at the top-right corner of the page, then clicking **Refresh**.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/preview on mobile.png>)
