---
title: Delete Snapshot
---


{% hint style="info" %}
**NOTE:** Once you delete a solution snapshot, you cannot restore it.
{% endhint %}

To delete a solution snapshot, in the solution configuration page, click the Solution Snapshots section header. The section expands.

Click the delete icon displayed in line with the snapshot you want to delete.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Delete snapshot_1.png>)

A confirmation pop-up appears. Click **Yes** to confirm deletion.

The solution snapshot is removed from the system and it no longer appears in the snapshots table.
