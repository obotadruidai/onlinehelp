---
title: Download Snapshot
---


Once a solution snapshot is generated, you can download and then import it in another AI Agent or in a different tenant.

To download a snapshot, in the solution configuration page, click the Solution Snapshots section header. The section expands.

Click the download icon (![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Download Snapshot.png>)) displayed in line with the snapshot you want to download.

The download archive pop-up appears. Choose to save the zip file and click **OK**. The zip file is saved on your machine to your default download folder.

You can import it in another AI Agent or in a different tenant by following the procedure [Importing Solutions](<Importing Solutions.md>).
