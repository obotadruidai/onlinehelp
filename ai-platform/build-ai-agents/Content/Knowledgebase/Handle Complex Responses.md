---
title: "Handle Complex Responses"
---

<a id="HandleComplexResponses"></a>


KB authors might identify that a question requires a more complex response, like getting information from an application or having multiple variants for the response. When you identify that a complex response is required to an existing simple question, follow these steps:

1. Make sure that you have the permission to manage flows. Get in touch with your Druid Portal administrator to promote you from KB author to advanced flow author.
2. From the QnA, select the KB article that needs a complex response. The Edit QnA page appears.
3. At the top right corner of the page, click the **Convert to Flow** button.
4. In the confirmation modal, click **Yes**.

The QnA refreshes, the QnA flow becomes a normal flow and it no longer appears under the QnA. To make a more complex flow authoring, go to **Flows**. The flow is marked with the label QnA.
