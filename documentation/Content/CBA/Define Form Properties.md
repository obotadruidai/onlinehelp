# Define Form Properties

To customize a form’s properties:

1. On the entity’s Forms page, click the Edit icon (![](../Resources/Images/CBA/edit%20icon.png)) next to the form you want to modify. The Form Properties panel appears on the right side of the editor by default.
2. If your form contains multilingual entity fields, enter the form name and description in all languages configured on the AI Agent. This ensures business users see the form details in their selected language.

![](../Resources/Images/CBA/form%20properties.png)

3. Configure additional settings:

- **Open in full screen** – Opens the form expanded to full screen.
- **Read-only** – Prevents users from editing the form.

4. Click the **Save** icon (![](../Resources/Images/CBA/save%20view%20icon.png)) in the top-right corner of the editor to apply your changes.
