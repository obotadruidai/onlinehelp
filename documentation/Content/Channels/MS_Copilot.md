# MS Copilot

The Druid AI Platform platform serves as the foundation for adapting Microsoft Copilot to your organization’s specific needs, enabling the development and deployment of intelligent agents across your enterprise.

Copilot can access your organization’s resources, such as SharePoint and databases, to retrieve and use relevant information efficiently. When you integrate Druid AI Agents with Microsoft Copilot, you unlock additional business skills within the Copilot, enabling it to perform specialized business tasks and provide more context-aware assistance across your organization.

With this integration, the Druid-enabled Copilot becomes the central conversational platform for employees, offering:

- Easy access to advanced business skills.
- Improved team collaboration.
- A flexible foundation that adapts to evolving business requirements and future developments.
