## Healthcare

<a id="AppointmentManagementAgent"></a>

### Appointment Management Agent

- **EMIS Integration Skill**. Enhance the existing Appointment Management Agent with direct EMIS Health integration. This new capability allows the agent to interact directly with EMIS clinical diaries in real time, expanding its booking workflow to natively support primary care and clinical scheduling ecosystems.

<a id="Insurance"></a>

## Insurance

<a id="CustomerSelfServiceAgent"></a>

### Customer Self-Service Agent

- **Scheduling Appointments Skill** - The Scheduling Appointments Skill automates the process of booking, confirming, and managing appointments between reinsurance brokers, underwriters, and clients. By removing the manual effort of calendar coordination, it accelerates deal cycles, reduces scheduling friction, and ensures that high-value meetings are confirmed promptly and consistently — even across complex, multi-party scheduling requirements.

<a id="HR"></a>

## HR

<a id="EmployeeAgent"></a>

### Employee Agent

- **Overtime Requests Skill** - The Overtime Requests Skill extends the Employee Agent to handle the full lifecycle of overtime request management, from employee submission through to manager approval and payroll notification. By automating this process, it reduces approval turnaround times, ensures policy compliance, and creates a transparent audit trail for all overtime activity — benefiting both employees and HR administrators.

<a id="CaseManagement"></a>

## Case Management

<a id="CaseManagementAgent"></a>

### Case Management Agent

- **ServiceNow Integration Skill** - The ServiceNow Integration Skill connects the case management platform directly with ServiceNow, enabling cases, tickets, and incidents to flow automatically between systems without manual re-entry. This skill ensures that service teams working across both platforms remain fully synchronized, reducing resolution times and eliminating the risk of cases being missed or duplicated during handoff.
