## Healthcare

<a id="OutboundPatientEngagementAgentNewSkills"></a>

### Outbound Patient Engagement Agent — New Skills

- **Annual Physical Skill** - The Annual Physical Skill automates outreach to patients who are due or overdue for their annual wellness visit, using personalized messaging to encourage scheduling. It helps healthcare organizations improve preventive care compliance rates, meet quality benchmarks, and maintain stronger ongoing relationships with their patient population.
- **Test Results Skill** - This skill notifies patients when their test results are available and, where appropriate, provides a summary or directs them to the relevant clinical resource for follow-up. It reduces inbound call volumes related to results enquiries while ensuring patients receive timely, accurate information to support their next steps.

<a id="Insurance"></a>

## Insurance

<a id="LeadCapturingAgent"></a>

### Lead Capturing Agent

- **Salesforce Integration Skill** - The Salesforce Integration Skill connects the Lead Capturing Agent directly to the organization’s Salesforce CRM, ensuring that all captured leads, contact records, and interaction histories are automatically synchronized without any manual input. This skill eliminates data silos between the engagement platform and the sales team's primary system of record, enabling faster follow-up and more accurate pipeline reporting.

<a id="HR"></a>

## HR

<a id="RecruitingAgent"></a>

### Recruiting Agent

- **Referral Management Skill** - The Referral Management Skill enables employees to submit candidate referrals, track the status of their submissions, and receive updates on outcomes — all through the agent interface. For HR teams, it streamlines the intake and management of referral candidates, helping organizations tap into one of the most cost-effective and high-quality talent sourcing channels available.

<a id="CaseManagement"></a>

## Case Management

<a id="CaseManagementAgent"></a>

### Case Management Agent

- **ServiceNow Integration Skill** - The ServiceNow Integration Skill connects the case management platform directly with ServiceNow, enabling cases, tickets, and incidents to flow automatically between systems without manual re-entry. This integration ensures that service teams working across both platforms remain fully synchronized, reducing resolution times and eliminating the risk of cases being missed or duplicated during handoff.
